# 7 C.F.R. § 3560.852 — Requirements.

When required to submit a credit report under any provision of this part, such submission must include a current comprehensive credit report for both the entity and the individual principals, partners, members, and the individual sub-entities or natural persons who are responsible for controlling the ownership and operations of the applicant entity, including but not limited to, principals, partners, or members. The Agency will also accept combination comprehensive credit reports which provides a comprehensive view of the applicant's credit profile by combining data from all three major credit bureaus (Experian, Equifax, and TransUnion).
