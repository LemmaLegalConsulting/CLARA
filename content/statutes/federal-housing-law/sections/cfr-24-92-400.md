# 24 C.F.R. § 92.400 — Coordinated Federal support for housing strategies.

(a) General. HUD will provide assistance in accordance with Subtitle C of the Act.

(b) Notice of funding. HUD will publish a notice in the Federal Register announcing the availability of funding under this section as appropriate.
