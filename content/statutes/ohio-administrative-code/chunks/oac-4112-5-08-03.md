# Ohio Adm.Code 4112-5-08 — Discrimination in the employment of the disabled. (H)

## Source text

(2) Approval by an agency of the United States government of an employer's affirmative action plan that is required by federal law, does not relieve such employer of the obligations imposed by Chapter 4112. of the Revised Code, as it relates to employment of persons with a disability but such plans will be treated as voluntary plans for the purposes of paragraph (H)(1) of this rule.
