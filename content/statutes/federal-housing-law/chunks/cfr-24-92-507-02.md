# 24 C.F.R. § 92.507 — Closeout. (c)

## Source text

(5) Monitoring and enforcement of the requirements for all HOME-assisted units set forth in this part for the period of affordability specified in the HOME written agreement with the property owner;

(6) Compliance with use of program income, recaptured funds, and repayments in accordance with § 92.503. If the jurisdiction is not a participating jurisdiction (as a metropolitan city, urban county, State, consortium, or consortium member) when it receives funds, the funds are not subject to the requirements of this part;

(7) Compliance with the requirement in 2 CFR 200.345(a)(2) that the participating jurisdiction return any funds due as a result of a later refund, corrections, or other transactions including final indirect cost rate adjustments; and

(8) Compliance with the audit requirements at 2 CFR part 200, subpart F).
